## PART 1 — SECTIONS

*Working title used throughout the spec: **The Quiet Engine — A Practical Playbook for Building a Product That Sells Itself** (9 chapters, 214 pages). If the title changes, only three strings change: hero headline, offer name, and document title/meta.*

**1. Hero (`#hero`)** — Above the fold, single screen on desktop.
Must contain: ebook cover art (3D mockup, right or below on mobile); eyebrow label ("NEW — Launch Edition"); H1 headline; one-sentence subhead; 2-line supporting body; primary CTA button ("Get The Quiet Engine — $29"); secondary text link ("Preview the table of contents"); microcopy row of 5 items (214 pages, PDF/EPUB/Kindle, instant delivery, 30-day guarantee, free lifetime updates); a floating "launch price ends Nov 12, 2026" badge; one-click Apple/Google Pay + credit card icons row.

**2. Outcomes (`#outcomes`)** — "What changes in the first 30 days."
Must contain: section headline + subhead; 4 outcome cards in a 2×2 (desktop) / 1-column (mobile) grid, each with an icon, a result headline, 2-line body, and a "time to do it: ~1 hour" tag; a static horizontal stat strip under the cards (3 stats with numbers and labels).

**3. Inside the book (`#inside`)** — Table of contents.
Must contain: section headline + subhead; numbered list of all 9 chapters, each with title, one-line promise, and 2–3 sub-bullets of what is inside; a "format details" spec strip (page count, formats, diagrams/tables count, template count); a CTA button repeating the offer.

**4. Who it's for (`#fit`)** — Qualification, both directions.
Must contain: two side-by-side blocks — "This book is for you if" (5 checkmarked statements) and "It's not for you if" (3 statements); a one-line qualifier under each list ("If you read every point on the left and felt your chest tighten, this was written for you."); a "Still unsure?" mini-CTA to the FAQ anchor, not a second buy button.

**5. Author (`#author`)** — Credibility.
Must contain: author headshot (circular, 160px); author name; title/role; 3-line bio in first person; 3 credential rows (ex-company / shipped-product / readers-community), each with a short label and detail; a "signed" handwritten-style signature image; a link "More from <author>".

**6. Social proof (`#proof`)** — Proof before offer.
Must contain: a 4-stat row (readers, average rating, review count, regions); a "featured in" logo row (4 publications, grayscale with hover color); 3 testimonial cards (quote, name, role, company, star rating); a "rated X/5 from N reviews" line with a link to a reviews page; one long-form pull quote (32 words max) on a brand-tinted band.

**7. Offer & pricing (`#offer`)** — What they get and what it costs.
Must contain: 5-item "What you get" checklist (price-anchored contents); pricing block with **two products**: (a) ebook — $49, launch $29, 3 bullet features, "Add to cart"; (b) bundle "Full Engine Pack" — $129, launch $79, 6 bullet features, "Get the bundle", marked "Best value" with a badge; below it a 3-bonus row (bonus templates, bonus workshop recording, bonus quarterly update calls) each labeled "Free with launch"; price-anchor strikethrough line ("$199 value"); guarantee strip (30 days, no questions, refund, one line each); launch deadline line with date and a live countdown that freezes after Nov 12, 2026 with substitute copy ("Launch pricing has ended — standard price below").

**8. FAQ (`#faq`)** — Objections.
Must contain: 7 question accordion items (accordion, not modals; first item open by default): format and delivery, devices/readers, refund policy, time to implement, whether it replaces hiring, updates and editions, and team license; each answer 2–3 sentences; a contact link under the accordion; a repeat primary CTA button below it.

**9. Final CTA (`#final-cta`)** — Close.
Must contain: darkened brand band; headline; one-line restatement of the outcome ("60 pages from here to your first evergreen channel"); primary CTA button identical to the hero CTA (including price); guarantee microcopy; a plain-text fallback email link ("Prefer email? Send me the link"); a one-line legal/disclosure line.
