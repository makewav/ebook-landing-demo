Whether The Quiet Engine is for you

This book is for you if

  • You're the founder, head of product, or head of marketing at a company under 30 people.
  • You have real customers, but growth is manual and you own it.
  • You don't have a growth team, a brand budget, or a year to wait on a roadmap.
  • You've shipped something and you're tired of the second month being flat.
  • You want a system you can run solo, this quarter.

If you read every point on the left and felt your chest tighten, this was written for you.

It's not for you if

  • You're pre-revenue and looking for a complete go-to-market from zero.
  • You're a funded enterprise with a demand-gen team and a brand budget.
  • You want a step-by-step ad playbook — this book assumes organic, product-led, and word-of-mouth channels.

If you're on the right, save yourself the $29 and hire a consultant for a week.
